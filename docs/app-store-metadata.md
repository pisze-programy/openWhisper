## Description

> A block of plain text (no HTML). ~1600 chars recommended max.

```
OpenWhisper is private, on-device speech-to-text for your Mac. Hold a hotkey, speak, and release — your words appear as text in whatever app you're using. No account, no cloud transcription, no upload of your audio.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Dictate anywhere — press and hold the dictation hotkey, speak, release. Text lands in the focused app, or on your clipboard.
• AI formatting — rewrite transcripts in a style you choose: Formal, Casual, Minimal, or Brief. Runs through OpenRouter only when you turn it on, using your own API key.
• Translation — translate a dictated note into English, Polish, or other targets, with the same opt-in OpenRouter flow.
• Timeline history — every dictation is saved, grouped by day, tap to copy.
• Launch at login — OpenWhisper is ready the moment you sit down.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT
Speech is transcribed locally with an on-device model — your microphone audio never leaves your Mac. Your transcript text stays on your device too, unless you enable AI formatting or translation, in which case the text is sent to OpenRouter using an API key you provide. Anonymous, opt-in usage analytics report counters only, never your audio or text. Disable it in Settings at any time.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
• macOS 26 or later
• Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)

OpenWhisper is open source (Apache-2.0).
```
